MilkTrack Account Deletion

This page explains how users of the MilkTrack application can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  • Option 1: From the App
    Open MilkTrack → Go to Settings → Tap Delete Account
  • Option 2: Request via Email
    Send an email to:
    support@yourdomain.com
    Include your registered email or phone number so we can identify your account.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account will be permanently deleted
  • All personal data associated with your account will be removed
  • You will no longer be able to access the app using this account
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data Retention

After account deletion:

  • All personal data is deleted immediately or within a short processing time
  • Some non-personal data (such as logs or analytics) may be retained for up to 30 days
  • This retention is required for security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 1–3 business days after verification.
