Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect personal and technical information to provide a seamless milk management experience. This includes:

  • Identity Data: Name, Company Name, Phone Number, and Email.
  • Operational Data: Customer lists, milk quality parameters (FAT, SNF, Density, etc.), and delivery records.
  • Device Data: Information required to facilitate Bluetooth connectivity and Push Notifications.

2. Android Permissions and Usage

To function correctly on Android devices, MilkTrack requires the following permissions. Each is used strictly for the purposes described:

  • INTERNET: Required to sync data between Admin and Client devices and to store records in our secure cloud database.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to alert Clients of new milk entries and send Admins daily reminders.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required for the reliability of scheduled reminders and automated background tasks after a device restart.
  • BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN & CONNECT/SCAN: Essential for discovering, pairing, and receiving data from the Milk Analyzer hardware via BLE.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: On Android, this permission is a technical requirement for Bluetooth Scanning. We do not track or store your actual geographic coordinates.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Data is only shared with third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., Supabase, Firebase) as necessary to host the application services.
