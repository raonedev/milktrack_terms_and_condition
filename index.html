2. User Roles

MilkTrack operates with two types of users:

Admin Admin

  • Registers independently using phone number and email address.
  • Can create and manage customer accounts under their profile.
  • Can add daily milk data including FAT, SNF, protein, density, water, and weight.
  • Can mark delivery records for individual customers.
  • Is responsible for the accuracy of all data entered.

Customer Customer

  • Must be registered by an Admin before creating an account.
  • Registers using their phone number (which must already exist in the system) and email.
  • Can only view milk data and delivery records associated with their Admin.
  • Cannot modify any data.

4. Data and Privacy

MilkTrack collects and stores the following data to provide its services:

  • Personal information: name, phone number, email address

  • Delivery records: quantity in liters, delivery time, date

Data Access
